UPLOAD TO NETLIFY
1. Extract this ZIP on your computer.
2. In Netlify, open your new Sprynt40 site and choose the manual deploy / deploy folder option.
3. Upload the extracted folder contents so index.html is at the published root and the assets folder sits beside it.
4. Do not upload index.html alone. Do not rename the assets folder or move its files.
5. Open the published Netlify URL and test the videos and contact buttons.
